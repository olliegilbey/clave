## Important Discoveries

**THE BUG (root-caused, confirmed end to end, not yet fixed):** nav wedges
permanently when the elected executor is a starved background bar whose tab
positions are stale.

Mechanism, each step verified live:

1. Every newborn tab's bar fires an UNGATED birth `AnnounceVisit` (model.rs
   ~1188,1243). Phase 2's last commit made tab 5's bar the beacon holder.
2. All subsequent focus moves were native/beaconless (`clave open` focus,
   drive `focus_tab`), so tab 5's bar stayed executor while background —
   starved of frames (TabUpdate reaches only the active tab, FOOTGUNS).
3. Churn A closed tab 0 → real positions shifted down by one; the starved
   executor's set still says 6 tabs, tab 5 at position 5.
4. Nav press `{"row":1}`: executor correctly ranks tab 5 top (store ranking
   is broadcast-fresh; STORE said top=5, verified: tab_order={1:17…5:30}),
   emits `SwitchTab{position:5}` → `switch_tab_to(6)` → out of range →
   zellij silently no-ops (zero refusal lines in its log).
5. Its `AnnounceVisit{tab_id:5}` still broadcast (CLI twins visible in
   zellij log at 14:41:04.125-145 — the smoking gun that the executor DID
   act) → beacon re-names tab 5 → same stale executor re-elected → every
   further press dead. The documented "one stale press, one executor" trade
   (model.rs ~1740) assumed the press LANDS somewhere and heals; a refused
   switch heals nothing.

Fix direction (not yet designed in detail): the position-based
`switch_tab_to` is the rot. `Effect::FocusPane` via `focus_pane_with_id`
already exists one arm above (main.rs:87-90, "S2-proven nav… go_to_tab is a
known dead end") and is stable-id, position-immune. zellij-tile 0.44.3 has
NO switch-tab-by-id (only `switch_tab_to`, `go_to_tab`, `go_to_tab_name`).
Candidate: live-row pick → FocusPane on the tab's registered agent pane
(uuid_to_pane is broadcast truth), falling back to position only for tabs
with no registered pane. Needs TDD in model.rs tests + FOOTGUNS entry +
re-drive. Do NOT "fix" the script to anchor before the churn nav — the
script correctly models a user who last touched the bar in another tab; the
product is what's wrong.

Also: the worktree Bash guard refuses `$TMPDIR` interpolation and xargs;
zellij log lives at `/var/folders/dd/…/T/zellij-501/zellij-log/zellij.log`.
